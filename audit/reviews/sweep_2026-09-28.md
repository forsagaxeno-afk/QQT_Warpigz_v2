## 2. Findings by owning session

Each fix carries a `-- QQT_Warpigz_v3 X.Y.Z: why` marker. Each regression test must be shown to fail on 3.3.6: stash the fix, run the test, restore the fix. `STRICT` means `QQT_SWEEP_FINDINGS=1 QQT_SWEEP_FINDING=Fn QQT_SWEEP_STRICT=1` on the sweep file. It is a useful cross-check but does not replace the deterministic test. For S3 F8, whether STRICT really exits non-zero is unverified.

### 2.1 Helltide session (`HelltideRevamped/`), which matters most for S1

**H1. Road planner uses the nearest single lap (High, #1)**
- **Code:** `core/hr_roads.lua:149-186` `M.plan`. The start is `nearest(L,px,py,ROAD_MAX)` (cached) and the exit is `nearest(L,tx,ty,OFFROAD_MAX)`. The cost feeds `hr_chest_order.lua:235` and the switch rule at :326-331 (half-cost hysteresis, SWITCH_MAX 3 per 60 s). The `DETOUR_MAX` fallback at :237-240 only applies within `RECALL_MAX` of 150 m.
- **Evidence:** on the real `waypoints/jirandai.lua`, from 4 spots 13–15 m apart, the cost to chest A is 409 / 2179 / 2164 / 2179 m against a best route of 268–285 m (up to 7.8×), and chest A vs B flips at every step. On seed 1, 3 switches in 4 s, then the far chest stayed pinned 56 s and 1,156 cinders were lost. The verifier reproduced it exactly; the sweep-wide aggregates were not rechecked.
- **Fix:** build start candidates as one closest point per lap within ROAD_MAX of the player, keeping the cache. Build exit candidates the same way near the chest, or `{exit_idx}` when it is valid, dropping any where `crosses_out`. Take the minimum over pairs of `d(p,a)+min arc(a,b)+d(b,t)`. Optionally cap road/straight at 2.5× past RECALL_MAX. The change stays inside `hr_roads.lua` and adds no locals to `helltide.lua`.
- **Test:** `test_helltide_roads_laps.lua`, on the real jirandai loop, spots P1–P4, chests A (-523,-609) and B (-740,-594). Assert cost/brute-force-best ≤ 1.5, |Δcost| − moved ≤ 100 m, and A cheaper than B at all four spots. Add a synthetic figure-eight case.

**H2. `loot_hold` stale window after a yield: the loss path (Medium, #5)**
- **Code:** `tasks/helltide.lua:3885-3905`. `loot_hold` runs at about :1678, before `credit_yield` (:1897), and `credit_yield` never shifts `_loot_since`. The trip empties the bag, so `items > _loot_items` cannot restart the window either.
- **Evidence:** on seed 5, a Mythic landed 2.7 m away during Rosie's cast. Rosie completed at 5325.8, and at 5326.0 HR logged `Looter busy 15s without progress — farming on`, after which the Mythic was lost. This is a new variant: the known Rosie cast-drop issue creates the situation and this defect makes the loss certain. The fix still matters after the Rosie fix, because the same thing follows any yield (revive, other task).
- **Fix:** inside `loot_hold`, right after `local now = ...`, credit gaps ≥ 0.5 s to `self._loot_since` and `self._loot_quiet` via `self._loot_seen_at`. Use self fields only. The verifier checked this with `test_integration_helltide`, `test_rosie_loot_waiting_1022` and `test_rosie_night_v3` on both runtimes.
- **Test:** `test_helltide_loot_hold_trip.lua`, ported from the `loot_hold_after_trip` repro. Assert the second drop is picked, and that `without progress — farming on` and `Another move took the player off` each appear 0 times.

**H3. Rift chest pair resolved by player distance (Medium, #11)**
- **Code:** `core/hr_tear_event.lua:715-728` `find_rift_chest_actor`. It filters by `pos_dist(ap,pos)<=10` but ranks by `dist(ap)`, the distance to the player. `H.RIFT_OPEN_CHEST` is at :1160-1186, and the loop is bounded only by `RUPTURE_MAX_S=300`.
- **Fix:** `local d = pos_dist(ap, pos)`, a one-line change that was verified. Optionally, prefer interactable actors on the first resolve.
- **Test:** `test_helltide_rift_chest_pair.lua`, with two PandemoniumChests at +1 m and +9 m. Assert both open, ≤ 3 "spotted" lines, no "took longer than 300s", and that the second interaction targets chest B.

**H4. :55 release of HR's own tear pause, then teleport in the same tick (Low, #15)**
- **Code:** `helltide.lua:3924-3932` `release_movement` → `hr_tear_event.release_holds('stopped')`. `search_helltide.lua:17-31` `loot_hold()` only asks `loot_guard.busy()`. `:343-347` does the idle town teleport.
- **Fix:** `release_holds` records `released_held_at` in the tracker table when a pause had held drops back; expose `M.recent_release(4)`. The search `loot_hold()` then holds while that is set and Rosie is not busy, inside the existing `LOOT_HOLD_MAX_S` of 20 s.
- **Test:** `test_helltide_hour_end_tear.lua`, standing in a tear at :54 with a Legendary 5 m away. Set :55 and assert the drop is picked and the player leaves within 25 s. Add a case with Rosie disabled, where the teleport must still fire.

**H5. Debug logging has no switch (LOW, the harness author's finding)**
- **Code:** `helltide.lua:376, 2393-2397, 3219-3226` (`[PATROL]`, `[NAV] mode=`, `[CHEST RECALL]`). It showed 207 SPAM hits in S1 and 56 in S4, about 1.5–3 lines/s.
- **Fix:** gate these lines behind a debug checkbox or a rate limit.
- **Test:** an S1-style run of 60 s asserting that each tag appears ≤ 20 times.

## 5. Harness gaps and Coordinator actions (`joint_host.lua` is Coordinator-owned)

**Diverging implementations to reconcile before merging.** Four branches changed `joint_host.lua` independently:
- **Widgets surviving a reload:** S1 `opts.reload_keeps_widgets`, S2 and S4 `h.reload(dir,{keep_widgets=true})`, S5 `opts.persist_widgets`. Pick one API. Without it, a chaos reload turns HR, Arkham, Reaper, WarPigs, WarPug and SilentRaven off and resets Rosie to 2 m, which produces false STALL and idle hits.
- **`opts.fast_globals`** (S1, S4): speed only; assignments to base names are no longer recorded.
- **`opts.target_range`** (S5): `get_near_target_list` ignored its range. The S5 author recommends making both it and persist_widgets the defaults after a full suite run.
- **`chaos.channel_drop_if`** (S1), and LEFT_DROP naming the caster (S1).
- **S2 fixes to fold in:** `loot_manager.any_item_around` should read ground items; `reset_all_dungeons` should eject; `h.remove_actor` only searches `h.P`; the LOOP monitor masks digits in place keys (`pit2.1` and `pit2.2` merge).

**Recommended host changes (these remove the false findings in §4):**
- A plugin's `request_move`, `force_move` or `interact_object` during a travel channel breaks it (S3 does this locally; S4 F1 needs it).
- Channel drops must be tied to a kill, or tagged "causeless" and classified as expected. None may be injected in the last 0.5 s of a channel.
- LEFT_DROP should respect Rosie's pickup distance (`check_want_item(item,false)`), keeping beyond-range Unique/Mythic as a separate rule.
- `I.limbo` should only fire with travel, or should restore a new instance, and not during a committed fight or in town.

**What cannot be modelled (needs a live log):**
- Enemies neither move nor attack. Static packs 12–15 m away keep Rosie's fight hold, and a boss out of 12 m range is never engaged (the Reaper hang question).
- Terrain is boxes and rectangular walls with no z levels; Limbo lasts a fixed 2 s; frames are 0.1 s.
- The Universal Rotation and ClickRevive stand-ins are guesses; in particular, whether a dash, evade or cast breaks a TP or waypoint channel.
- Navigator, Worldstone, Butler, Scavenger and TristramLoop are absent.
- QQT's real reload semantics: does `main_toggle` survive? Does a module cache reset match?
- The lazy stash (32 then 300), the glyph UI rules, the boss loot spread, altar re-arm timing and the Hell's Prize spawn rule are all models.
- Only 48 of 114 suite files use `joint_host` and are monitored. LEFT_DROP needs Rosie mode. Getters that change state are never polled.

**Run-condition notes:**
- In S1, the heavy loop ran on LuaJIT only, and the HEAD re-verification covered seeds 1–4 only.
- In S2, the full suite was not run.
- S1 incident: `pkill -x xargs` at about 15:55 UTC may have killed another session's test batch. If any session's batch died around then, re-run it.
