## Known limits

- A user pause combined with Alfred's own teleport trip still breaks the pause. This also happens on 9e01f67, and round 5 made one case better. Evidence: my probe r5-critic/probe_pause_alfred.lua, nine plugins, LuaJIT and Lua 5.4, results in the matching _head/_base .txt files. - Paused at wave 3: when Alfred takes the player to Temis, in_run turns false and WarPigs logs 'detected self-disable'. After Alfred's portal back, it re-enables HordeDev, overriding the pause, but it is still one horde. On 9e01f67 with 'Use teleport' on, this case gave 2 hordes. - Paused after the Council (quest already on TurnIn): the turn-in teleports out of BSK after Alfred's return. 0 chests and no Leave Dungeon, identical to 9e01f67. - Suggested follow-up: in dispatch.user_paused, keep a pause whose in_run turned false while C1 Alfred live work or a teleport cast lasts, capped at HORDE_TRIP_CAP (mirrors hwe_left_hold).
- A paused plugin whose in_run drops to false for another reason becomes a normal self-disable, and WarPigs re-enables it when its quest is still wanted. Examples: kicked out of the Pit or Undercity by the timer, or the pause taken during HordeDev's own Alfred trip, which also costs one extra Alfred trip (H5-3 note c). This is the behaviour from before W5-4.
- W5-4 has no WarPigs-side time bound, by design. The pause shows in the status line at once and is logged once; the C6 watchdog reports it at 60/120 s when it holds a handoff. Unticking the plugin's GUI main toggle is also reported as 'paused by its hotkey'.
- An ArkhamAsylum paused while running as the pit filler is re-enabled when a real ThePit quest appears (reason_changed). This is the auditor's edge; very low.
- W5-3 wording: a landing in the Horde zone inside a world without 'BSK' is reported as 'landed in a BSK zone HordeDev does not know (zone=S05_BSK_Prototype02, ...)'. Cosmetic; the line carries both names.
- The joint's K1 evidence 'War Plan objective already complete' rests on live assumptions: - arena_quiet (no is_enemy actor above 1 HP and no pylon interaction) must never last 20 s during waves of an adopted horde while another War Plan step is listed. - After a reload in a chest-room horde past the Council, open_chests must see the chests before 20 s of quiet, or the horde is left without chests.
- Legacy Alfred provider that publishes only need_trigger: Arkham and HelltideRevamped treat it as a hard need; WonderCity, Reaper and HordeDev treat it as advisory. Unchanged; the live Alfred publishes both booleans.
- By the policy, advisory flags are serviced only by WarPigs' Temis kick (nothing owned) or its preamble. An activity that passes through Temis while WarPigs owns it (for example Arkham from Kurast with 'Use teleport' off) gets no service; the flag waits for the next idle Temis visit, which is at least the turn-in.
- Compass-mode HordeDev with use_alfred off and Alfred loaded runs its built-in Cerrigar salvage from utils.is_inventory_full(), which counts need_trigger as full. Pre-existing, compass mode only.
- The '<plugin>: same-activity continuation OPT-OUT ... proceeding to full transition' line repeats every 0.6 s during a HordeDev handoff with 'Use teleport' on (about 35 lines in 20 s in my probe). Cosmetic, known since round 4.
- A War Plan teleport that lands in an already finished horde has no completion path (back-to-back Horde steps, or a Leave Dungeon that failed 3 times). W5-2(b) now enables in place there too. Pre-existing F-H1 behaviour; covered by live checklist item H10.
- Largest live unknown, unchanged: where warplan.teleport_to_activity() really lands for a Horde War Plan. With the defaults a gate landing is bounded and visible but never enters. With the fallback ticked and 'Use teleport' off it currently runs into blocker 1 once the bag has no compass.
